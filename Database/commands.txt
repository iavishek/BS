Meta-Commands (psql)


• \l (List all databases)
• \c (Attempted database connection, defaulted to postgres)
• \c company; (Successfully connected to the company database)
• \d (List tables in the database)
• help (Requested basic psql help text)
• \? (Showed help for all backslash commands)
• \d department (Viewed structural details of the department table)
• \d department; (Viewed structural details again)
• \d employee (Viewed structural details of the employee table)


SQL Queries


• SELECT * FROM department (Attempted without a semicolon)
• SELECT * FROM department; (Failed due to the broken multi-line buffer containing a forward slash)
